# Get started with First

First helps engaged couples in the US make a prenup together.

- For a general question about prenups or about First, call `ask_clara`. It works before the person signs in.
- When the person wants to start or continue a prenup, call `get_started`. First then asks the person to sign in or make an account, and to accept the consent page.
- Follow the instructions of the server and the descriptions of the tools.
- Payment, signing, and the lawyer review stay in the First app. Give the person the app link from the tool result.
- First gives general information, not legal advice.
